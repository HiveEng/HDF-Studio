# Revision clouds, polylines and polygons

## Revision clouds

Clouds can follow any outline, not just a rectangle.

![Click each corner, then right-click to finish](d-cloud.svg "plain")

1. Click **Cloud** on the Comment or Home tab, or press [[C]].
2. Click each corner around the change.
3. To finish, **right-click**, double-click, or press [[Enter]]. The cloud closes back to the first point.

For a quick rectangular cloud, drag from one corner to the other instead of clicking.

[Try it: Revision cloud](cmd:tool-cloud)

Points snap to the drawing's line ends and corners when [snapping](#measure/snapping-to-the-drawing) is on ([[F9]]); hold [[Alt]] to place a point freely.

> **Tip:** Press [[Backspace]] while drawing to remove the last point, if you clicked in the wrong place. Press [[Esc]] to cancel the whole cloud.

## Polylines and polygons

- **Polyline** ([[Shift+Y]]): click each point of an open line. Right-click, double-click or [[Enter]] to finish.
- **Polygon**: click each corner of a closed shape, then finish the same way.

## Changing them

Select with the Hand tool ([[V]]) and right-click for colour swatches, **Properties** (line width, opacity, colour, comment) and **Delete**. To restyle many clouds at once, select them together; see [Selecting and changing markups](#selecting).

Default colours and line widths for clouds and lines are in **Settings › Tools**.
