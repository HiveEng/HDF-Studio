# Comparing drawings and documents

**Compare files** ([[Ctrl+Shift+C]], on the Comment tab and the start screen) finds every change between an older and a newer version.

## Starting a comparison

- **With a document open:** click **Compare files**, then pick the other version from your open tabs or **Choose a file**. Say whether the open document is the newer or the older version.
- **With nothing open:** click **Compare files** and pick both files; then say which is older.

![Compare files](compare-dialog.png "small")

## Three ways to see the changes

| Show the changes as | What you get | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Change report** | A copy of the newer version with every change marked as a comment. The Comments panel lists them; click one to jump to it. | Documents, specifications, and drawings with text changes |
| **Overlay** | Both versions on top of each other in two colours: older red, newer blue (or green). What's the same turns dark; what moved or changed stands out in colour. | Drawings: moved walls, new doors, revised details |
| **Side by side** | Older and newer next to each other on one sheet. They zoom and scroll together. | Checking a revision against the previous issue |

![How the overlay colours read](d-compare.svg "plain")

![An overlay of two revisions: the moved partition and new door show in colour](compare-overlay.png "wide")

## Options

- **Overlay alignment:** *Automatic* lines up drawings that moved on the sheet (for example after a re-plot); *Page corners* or *Page centres* line them up by the sheet.
- **Overlay colours:** older red with newer blue, or older red with newer green.
- **Report: also compare images and drawings:** on pages whose text didn't change, also look for changes in the line work and pictures.

The result opens in a new tab, so the originals are untouched. Save it to keep it.

> **Tip:** Compare a scanned or SHX-lettered drawing as an **overlay**: the overlay compares what you see, so it works even when the PDF has no searchable text.
