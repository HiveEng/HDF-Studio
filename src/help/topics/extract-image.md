# Extracting images and areas

**Extract image** (Edit tab, Images group) saves either an embedded picture or any rectangle of the page.

1. Click **Edit › Extract image**.
2. **Click a picture** to save it at its original resolution, or **drag a box** around any part of the page (a detail, a title block, part of a plan) to save that area as a 300 dpi PNG.
3. Choose where to save the file. [[Esc]] cancels.

![Drag a box around any part of the drawing to save it as an image](d-extract.svg "plain")

> **Note:** CAD drawings are line work, not pictures, so clicking on them finds no picture. Drag a box instead and the area is saved exactly as you see it, markups included.
