# Find and replace, and spelling

## Find and replace

1. Click **Edit › Find & replace**, or press [[Ctrl+H]].
2. Type what to find and what to replace it with.
3. Click **Find next** to step through matches, **Replace** to change one, or **Replace all**.

Replacements are made in the page text and in comments. Changed paragraphs are rewritten in the nearest standard font and re-wrap within their column. [[Ctrl+Z]] undoes.

> **Important:** Find and replace changes the document itself. Check the result on drawings, where text often sits in tight title blocks.

## Spelling

Click **Spelling** (Comment or Edit tab) or press [[F7]].

- {{app}} checks your comments and text boxes, and the page text if you choose.
- Click a suggestion to fix the word, **Ignore** or **Ignore all** to skip it, or **Add to dictionary** to teach {{app}} a word such as a product name.
- Click **Check again** after more changes.

The English dictionary is built in, so spelling works offline. Words you add are kept on this computer.
