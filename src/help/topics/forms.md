# Filling forms

When a PDF has form fields, {{app}} says so in the status bar. Click a field and type, or tick check boxes and choose from lists. [[Tab]] moves to the next field.

Fields with calculations (totals, sums) update as you type. Date fields open a calendar, and buttons on the form clear it, print it or open a link.

## Fill from a spreadsheet

**Fill & Sign › Fill from CSV** makes one filled copy of the form for each row of a spreadsheet saved as CSV:

1. Make a spreadsheet whose column headings match the field names, and save it as CSV.
2. Click **Fill from CSV** and pick the file.
3. Choose how to name the output files, using column names in braces, for example `{Last name} - {Invoice}` (`{row}` is the row number), and whether to flatten the filled copies so the values are locked.
4. Click **Create files** and choose a folder.

## Other form commands

| Command | Does |
|---|---|
| **Export data** | Saves the answers as CSV |
| **Clear fields** | Empties every field |
| **Flatten** | Turns the fields into plain page content so they can't be changed |

To add fields or make a flat form fillable, see [Building forms](#form-builder).
