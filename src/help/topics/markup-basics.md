# Markup basics

The **Comment** tab holds every markup tool. The most-used ones are also on the Home tab.

![The Comment tab](ribbon-comment.png "wide")

![A revision cloud, a callout and a review stamp on a floor plan](markups.png "wide")

## How tools work

1. Pick a tool: click its button, or press its key (shown in the tooltip and below).
2. Click or drag on the page. The status bar always says what to do next.
3. The tool stays active so you can add more. Press [[Esc]] or [[V]] to go back to the Hand tool.

Every markup records your name as its author and appears in the [Comments panel](#review). Undo with [[Ctrl+Z]].

Press the key while the page has focus (not while typing in a box).

## Text markup

- **Highlight** ([[H]]), **Underline** ([[U]]) and **Strike out** ([[K]]): drag across the words.
- Or select text first with **Select** ([[T]]), then press [[H]], [[U]] or [[K]]. [[Ctrl+C]] copies the selected text.

On a scanned page, run [OCR](#ocr) first so there is text to mark.

## Notes and text

- **Sticky note** ([[N]]): click where the note goes, then type your comment in the box that opens. The page shows a small note icon; the text is in the Comments panel.
- **Text box** ([[X]]): click and type right on the page. See [Text boxes](#text-boxes).
- **Callout** ([[L]]): a text box with an arrow. See [Callouts and leaders](#callouts-leaders).

## Drawing

- **Pen** ([[P]]): draw freehand.
- **Rectangle** ([[R]]), **Ellipse** ([[O]]) and **Arrow** ([[A]]): drag from one corner (or end) to the other.
- **Cloud**, **Polyline** and **Polygon**: click point by point. See [Revision clouds and shapes](#clouds-shapes).

## Colour

The **Colour** box on the Comment tab sets the colour for new markups. Click **Default** to go back to each tool's own colour from [Settings](#settings).

To recolour a markup you've already drawn, right-click it and pick a swatch. Select several markups to recolour them all at once; see [Selecting and changing markups](#selecting).

## Editing a markup

With the Hand tool ([[V]]):

- Click a markup to select it. Text boxes, callouts, stamps, signatures and pictures can be dragged to move them, and have handles to resize or turn them.
- Press [[Enter]] to edit its text or comment (or double-click a text box or callout).
- Right-click for colours, **Properties** (comment, author, colour, line width, text size, opacity) and **Delete**.
- [[Delete]] removes the selected markup.

## Flattening

**Flatten** (Comment tab, Review group) burns markups into the page so they can't be moved or edited, which is useful before sending a final set. You can flatten comments, form fields, or both. [[Ctrl+Z]] undoes it until you close the file.
