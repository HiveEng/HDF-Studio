# Pages, zoom and panning

## The page and zoom bar

The bar in the middle of the footer works the same on every tab.

![Page and zoom controls in the footer](viewbar.png "small")

From left to right: previous page, page number (type a number and press [[Enter]]), page count, next page, zoom out, zoom level, zoom in, **Fit width** and **Fit page**.

## Zooming

| To | Do this |
|---|---|
| Zoom in or out | [[Ctrl+=]] and [[Ctrl+-]], or hold [[Ctrl]] and turn the mouse wheel |
| Fit the page width to the window | **Fit width** or [[Ctrl+2]] |
| See the whole page | **Fit page** or [[Ctrl+0]] |
| Actual size (100%) | [[Ctrl+1]] |

On a touchpad, pinch to zoom.

## Panning

You can pan with any tool, so you never lose your place mid-markup:

- **Middle mouse button:** press and drag.
- **Space bar:** hold [[Space]] and drag with the left button (Hand tool).
- **Mouse wheel:** scrolls up and down; [[Shift]] and the wheel scrolls sideways.
- **Hand tool** ([[V]]): drag on a part of the page with no markup. Note that with the Hand tool, a drag on empty space draws a selection window instead; see [Selecting markups](#selecting).

## Moving between pages

| To | Do this |
|---|---|
| Next or previous page | [[Page Down]] and [[Page Up]], or the arrows in the footer |
| First or last page | [[Home]] and [[End]] |
| Go to a page | [[Ctrl+G]], type the number, press [[Enter]] |
| Jump to a page | Click its thumbnail in the **Pages** panel, or a bookmark in **Bookmarks** |
| Go back after following a link | [[Alt+Left]] |

## Showing more of the page

- [[F4]] hides or shows the sidebar.
- On a small screen, a narrow window keeps the ribbon usable by shrinking its buttons; see [A tour of the window](#interface).
