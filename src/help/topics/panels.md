# Sidebar panels

The rail on the left edge shows one panel at a time in the sidebar. [[F4]] hides or shows the sidebar.

| Icon | Panel | Use it to |
|---|---|---|
| Grid | **Pages** | See thumbnails, select pages, drag them to reorder, and rotate, insert, extract or delete them with the buttons at the top. |
| List | **Bookmarks** | Jump to a section, and add, rename, reorder or delete bookmarks. |
| Magnifier | **Search results** | See every match of your last search. |
| Speech bubbles | **Comments** | See every markup and comment, reply, set review status and filter. See [Comments and review](#review). |
| Paper clip | **Attached files** | Open, save, add or remove files embedded in the PDF. |
| Stacked sheets | **Layers** | Show or hide drawing layers. |
| Toolbox | **Tool chest** | One-click saved markup tools. See [Tool chest and saved styles](#tool-chest). |

## Pages

- Click a thumbnail to go to that page. [[Ctrl]]+click adds or removes a page from the selection; [[Shift]]+click selects a run of pages. [[Up]] and [[Down]] move through pages; [[Ctrl+A]] selects all.
- Drag thumbnails to change the page order. Drop PDF files from File Explorer between thumbnails to insert them there.
- Buttons at the top of the panel act on the selected pages (or the current page): rotate left [[Ctrl+[]], rotate right [[Ctrl+]]], insert a blank page, insert pages from a file, extract to a new tab, and delete ([[Delete]]).

See [Organizing pages](#organize) for more.

## Bookmarks

Click a bookmark to jump to it. Right-click a bookmark for:

- **Go to**, **Rename** ([[F2]]), **Set to the current view** and **Link to a web address**
- **Add bookmark after** and **Add sub-bookmark**
- **Indent** and **Outdent** to change the level
- **Delete** ([[Delete]])

[[Ctrl+D]] bookmarks the current view.

## Attached files

PDFs can carry other files inside them, such as calculations, specifications, CAD files or photos. Use **Attach file** on the Edit tab to add one. Right-click an attachment to **Save a copy**, **Replace with another file** or **Remove from PDF**.

## Layers

Drawings exported from CAD often keep their layers. Tick or untick a layer to show or hide it, or use **Show all** and **Hide all**. Layer visibility is saved with the file.
