# Comments and review

## The Comments panel

Click the speech bubbles on the panel rail, or **Comment › Comments**, to list every markup and comment in the document, page by page.

![The Comments panel beside a marked-up drawing](comments.png "wide")

- Click a comment to jump to it on the page and select it.
- **Reply** to start a conversation on a comment.
- **Set status** to Accepted, Rejected, Cancelled, Completed or back to Open. The status shows in the list and in the exported markup list.
- **Filter by author** and **Filter by status** to see, for example, only the open comments from one reviewer.
- **Copy summary** copies a text summary of all comments to the clipboard, ready to paste into an email or report.

Set the name shown on your comments in [Settings](#settings).

## Merging reviewers' comments

When several people review copies of the same drawing:

1. Open your copy.
2. Click **Comment › Merge reviews**.
3. Pick the reviewers' PDF copies or XFDF comment files. Their comments are added to your copy, each with its reviewer's name.

## Exporting the markup list

**Comment › Export list** (or **Measure › Export totals**) saves every markup with its page, type, author, status, comment and measured value.

![Export markup list](export-list.png "small")

- **Excel workbook (.xlsx)** includes a *Totals* sheet that adds up measurements by type and page, ready for a quantity takeoff.
- **CSV** opens in any spreadsheet.

## Checking spelling

**Spelling** ([[F7]]) checks your comments and text boxes, and can check the page text too. It uses a built-in English dictionary, so it works offline. Click a suggestion to fix a word, or add the word to your dictionary (kept on this computer).

## Comparing revisions

To see what changed between two versions of a drawing, use **Compare files**. See [Comparing drawings and documents](#compare).
