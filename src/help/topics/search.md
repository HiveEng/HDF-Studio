# Searching a document

1. Click **Search document** at the top right, or press [[Ctrl+F]].
2. Type a word or phrase and press [[Enter]].
3. Every match is highlighted on the pages, and the **Search results** panel lists them with the words around each one. Click a result to jump to it.
4. Press [[F3]] for the next match and [[Shift+F3]] for the previous one, or use the arrows beside the search box.

![Search results for "office", listed in the sidebar and highlighted on the sheet](search.png "wide")

The count of matches and the time the search took appear in the status bar.

## When nothing is found

- **Scanned pages** have no text until it's recognized. Run [OCR](#ocr) first.
- **CAD drawings** often draw lettering as lines (AutoCAD SHX fonts), which isn't searchable text. Run [OCR](#ocr) on those pages to make the lettering searchable.

> **Tip:** To search the guide instead, press [[F1]] and type in the box at the top of the guide.
