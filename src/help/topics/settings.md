# Settings and your preferences

Open Settings with the gear at the top right, or press [[Ctrl+,]].

![Settings, General tab](settings.png "small")

## General

| Setting | What it changes |
|---|---|
| **Your name** | The author shown on comments, markups and review stamps. Filled in from your Microsoft 365 sign-in. |
| **Company** | Printed on review stamps. |
| **Measurement units** | Feet and inches, decimal feet, inches, metres, millimetres or centimetres. Used when you set a scale. |
| **Measurement precision** | How measurements are rounded. The list follows the units: fractions (nearest 1", 1/2", 1/4", 1/8" or 1/16") for feet and inches, and 0 to 4 decimal places for the others. *Usual* is 1/16" for feet and inches. |
| **Date format** | 2026-11-25, 11/25/2026 or November 25, 2026, used on stamps. |
| **Theme** | Light or dark. |
| **OCR language** | The language text recognition expects. |
| **Print size and quality** | Your usual choices for the Print dialog. |

> **Tip:** To change the precision of a single measurement, right-click it and choose **Measurement text (size, precision)**. See [Measuring](#measure).

## Tools

The **Tools** tab sets the colour, line width, opacity and text size that new markups start with, for each kind of tool: rectangles, ellipses and arrows; the pen; text boxes; revision clouds; callouts; polylines and polygons; dimension lines; measurements; and count markers. **Reset tool defaults** puts them back.

The colour picker on the Comment tab overrides the colour for the moment; choose **Default** there to go back to each tool's own colour.

You can also save a style straight from a markup: right-click a text box or callout, choose **Style**, and under **Use this style** pick *For this one and every new …*. See [Tool chest and saved styles](#tool-chest).

## Where settings are kept

Settings, your signature, saved styles, your spelling dictionary and recent files are kept for your Windows account on this computer. To use the same markup tools on another computer, export your [tool chest](#tool-chest) and import it there.
