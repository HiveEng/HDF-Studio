# Signing with your signature

![The Fill & Sign tab](ribbon-sign.png "wide")

## Creating your signature

The first time you sign, {{app}} asks for your signature. To make a new one later, click **Fill & Sign › New signature**.

![Draw, type or import your signature](signature.png "small")

- **Draw:** sign with the mouse, a pen or your finger. **Clear** starts again.
- **Type:** type your name and pick a style (Script, Handwritten or Formal).
- **Image:** pick a photo or scan of your signature, paste one with [[Ctrl+V]], or drop it on the box. Leave **Remove background** ticked for photos and scans, so only the ink shows.

Click **Use signature**. It's saved on this computer only.

## Placing your signature

1. Click **Sign** (Home or Fill & Sign tab), or press [[G]].
2. Click on the page where the signature goes.

To sign a form's signature field, drag the field's **Sign here** onto it.

## Moving, sizing and turning

Click the signature with the Hand tool ([[V]]):

- Drag to move it; drag a corner to resize it (hold [[Shift]] to stretch).
- Drag the round knob to turn it.
- Right-click for **Rotate right 90°**, **Rotate left 90°**, **Rotate 180°** and **Size and rotation**.

Signatures go on upright on rotated CAD sheets.

> **Important:** A placed signature is a picture on the page, and can be moved until you **Flatten** (Comment or Fill & Sign tab). For a signature that proves who signed and that the file hasn't changed since, use a [digital ID](#digital-ids).
