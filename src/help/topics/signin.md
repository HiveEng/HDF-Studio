# Signing in and updates

## Signing in

{{app}} is for Hive Engineering staff. The first time it starts on a computer, it asks you to sign in with your work Microsoft 365 account.

1. Click **Sign in with Microsoft**.
2. Your web browser opens. Sign in as usual, including any multi-factor prompt.
3. Come back to {{app}}. It unlocks and shows your name in the status bar.

Your password never passes through {{app}}: the browser handles the sign-in.

After that you stay signed in on that computer. {{app}} quietly confirms your account in the background, and keeps working offline for up to 30 days. If it can't reach Microsoft for longer, it asks you to connect and sign in again.

| To | Do this |
|---|---|
| See who is signed in | [[Ctrl+K]], then *Account* |
| Sign out | [[Ctrl+K]], then *Sign out* |

Your name from the sign-in is used as the author of your comments and stamps. You can change it in [Settings](#settings).

## Updates

{{app}} checks for a new version a few seconds after it starts. When there is one, it asks whether to update now. Save your work first: the app restarts on the new version, with your settings kept.

To check yourself, click **? › Check for updates**, or use **Settings › About**.

If you skip an update, use **Check for updates** when you're ready.

## Installing on a new computer

Download **HDS-Setup.exe** from the release page and run it. It installs for your Windows account, so it doesn't need administrator rights. IT can deploy **HDS.msi** instead.

> **Note:** The installer isn't code-signed yet. If Windows shows *Windows protected your PC*, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
