# Review stamps

1. Click **Stamp** on the Comment tab, or press [[S]].
2. Pick a stamp, or choose **Custom text** and type your own (it's shown in capitals) and pick its colour.
3. Click on the page where the stamp goes.

Each stamp shows its title, your name, the date and your company. Set your name, company and date format in [Settings](#settings).

| Stamp | Colour |
|---|---|
| APPROVED, APPROVED AS NOTED, REVIEWED - NO EXCEPTIONS TAKEN, FOR CONSTRUCTION | Green |
| REVIEWED, FOR REVIEW, RECEIVED | Blue |
| MAKE CORRECTIONS NOTED, REVISE AND RESUBMIT, PRELIMINARY - NOT FOR CONSTRUCTION | Orange |
| REJECTED, VOID | Red |

{{app}} remembers the last stamp you used.

## Moving, resizing and turning a stamp

Click the stamp with the Hand tool ([[V]]):

- Drag to move it.
- Drag a corner to resize it (hold [[Shift]] to stretch freely).
- Drag the round knob to turn it.
- Right-click for **Rotate right 90°** ([[]]]), **Rotate left 90°** ([[[]]), **Rotate 180°** and **Size and rotation** (exact width, height and angle).

Stamps go on upright on rotated CAD sheets, the way you see the page.

> **Tip:** Save a stamp to the [tool chest](#tool-chest) to place it with one click, and share the chest with your team so everyone stamps the same way.
