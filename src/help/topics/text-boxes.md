# Text boxes

## Adding a text box

1. Click **Text box** on the Comment tab, or press [[X]].
2. Click on the page where the text should start.
3. Type. Press [[Enter]] for a new line.
4. Press [[Ctrl+Enter]] or click elsewhere to finish. [[Esc]] cancels.

The box grows to fit what you type.

## Moving, resizing and turning

Select the text box with the Hand tool ([[V]]) to show its grips.

![A selected text box: drag inside to move, corner and side grips to resize, the round knob to turn it](textbox-grips.png "small")

![Text box grips](d-textbox-grips.svg "plain")

- **Move:** drag anywhere inside the box.
- **Resize:** drag a corner or side grip. The text re-wraps to the new width.
- **Turn:** drag the round knob above the box. Hold [[Shift]] to turn in 15° steps.
- **Edit the text:** double-click the box, or press [[Enter]].

## Styling

Right-click the text box and choose **Style (colour, fill, size, arrow)**.

| Option | Choices |
|---|---|
| **Text** | The words in the box. |
| **Text and border colour** | Any colour. |
| **Text size** | In points. A bigger size makes the box bigger. |
| **Fill** | None (see-through), White, Light yellow or a custom colour. |
| **Text alignment** | Left, Centre or Right. Widen the box first to give centred or right-aligned text room. |
| **Border width** | In points; 0 for no border. |
| **Use this style** | *For this one only*, *For this one and every new text box*, or *For this one, and save it to the tool chest*. |

Restyling never narrows a box you've widened.

> **Tip:** Save your office's standard note style once with *every new text box*, and every text box after that starts with it. Save several styles (for example *General note 10 pt* and *Title 14 pt*) to the [tool chest](#tool-chest) to switch between them with one click.

## Adding leaders to a text box

Right-click the text box and choose **Add leader**, then click each point the leaders should point to. Press [[Esc]] or [[Enter]] when done. See [Callouts and leaders](#callouts-leaders).

## Text boxes on rotated sheets

On a page rotated in the PDF (common with CAD sheets plotted in landscape), new text boxes, stamps and signatures go on upright, the way you see the sheet.
