# Welcome to {{app}}

{{app}} (Hive Document Studio) is Hive Engineering's PDF reader and editor, built for engineering and CAD drawings as well as everyday documents. It opens quickly, works offline, and keeps every tool one click or one key away.

![The {{app}} window with a floor plan open](window.png "wide")

## Three ways to find any tool

1. **The ribbon.** Tabs across the top are named after jobs (Comment, Measure, Protect…). Hover over any button to see what it does and its shortcut.
2. **Ctrl+K.** Press [[Ctrl+K]] (or click **Tools** at the top right) and type a few letters, such as *cloud* or *bates*. Press [[Enter]] to run the highlighted command. Commands you use most come first.
3. **Right-click.** Right-click the page, a markup, a page thumbnail or a bookmark for the commands that apply to it.

![Ctrl+K finds every command by name, with its shortcut](palette.png "small")

## Getting help

- Press [[F1]] at any time. The guide opens at the topic for the tab or tool you're using.
- Click the **?** button at the top right for the guide, keyboard shortcuts and updates.
- Type in the search box at the top of this window. Results show the matching section with your words highlighted; press [[Enter]] to open the first one, or use the arrow keys.
- Every guide topic is also listed in [[Ctrl+K]] as *Help: …*.

> **Tip:** Links like [Try it: Revision cloud](cmd:tool-cloud) close the guide and start that tool for you.
