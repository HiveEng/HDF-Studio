# What's new

## User guide

- **This guide.** Press [[F1]] for help on the tool or tab you're using, or click **?** at the top right. Search the whole guide, with figures, diagrams and screenshots of every feature. Guide topics also appear in [[Ctrl+K]].

## Recent improvements

- **Window and crossing selection** of markups, like AutoCAD: drag left to right for markups fully inside, right to left for anything the box touches. [[Shift]]+click and [[Ctrl]]+click add or remove; [[Ctrl+A]] selects all on the page. Delete or restyle many at once. See [Selecting](#selecting).
- **Pan anywhere** with the middle mouse button or [[Space]]+drag.
- **Leaders** start at the box edge facing the arrow, and free leaders draw tip first. See [Callouts and leaders](#callouts-leaders).
- **Text alignment** (left, centre, right) for text boxes and callouts, kept in saved styles.
- **Stacked fractions** in feet-and-inch measurements and dimensions. See [Measuring](#measure).
- **Page and zoom controls** in the middle of the footer on every tab.
- **Undo and Redo** beside the ribbon tabs on every tab.
- **Measurement precision** that follows your units: decimals for metric and decimal feet, fractions for feet and inches.
- **OCR on the Home tab.**
- **Polygon revision clouds**, **turnable and resizable text boxes**, **editable measurement text**, **right-click to finish** multi-point tools.
- **Extract image** of a picture or any area of the page, and **find and redact AutoCAD SHX text**.
- **Open and save with Microsoft 365**: OneDrive, Recent and Shared with me.
